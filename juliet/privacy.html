Privacy Policy

App: Juliet — Photocard Maker (shown as "Juliet Card" on the home screen; package name: com.eruclarissa.juliet)
Developer: ERU CLARISSA
Last updated: November 2, 2026

Overview

Juliet ("the app") is an app for making and printing your own photocards, developed by ERU CLARISSA. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The app does not require an account and does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send your photos or other data anywhere. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

Photos you choose

When you add a photo, you choose it with Android's photo picker. The app can read only the photos you select; it does not read the rest of your photo library and does not ask for permission to access it. You can also send images to the app from other apps with Android's Share feature; the app receives only the images you share.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, in storage that other apps cannot read: the sheets you make (the photos you added, how each photo is cropped and placed, the paper and card sizes, decorations such as frames, names, messages and colors), the sheet you are working on, and your settings such as the display language. This information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Saving, sharing and printing

If you choose "Save to Photos", the app saves the images it makes (sheets, cards and announcement images) to your device's photo gallery. On Android 10 and earlier, this uses the storage permission only for saving these images. If you choose "Share" or "Send to print app", the image is passed to the app you select through Android's share sheet. If you choose "Print (PDF)", the sheet is passed to Android's printing system and the printer or print service you select. From that point, the receiving app or service handles the image under its own policy. The app does not upload anything by itself.

QR codes in announcement images

If you add a QR code to an announcement image, the app makes the QR code on your device from the text you type, or cuts it out of an image you choose. The text and image are not sent anywhere.

Ratings and reviews

After you have saved or shared a few sheets, the app may show Google Play's in-app review window so that you can rate the app without leaving it. "Rate the app" in the settings opens the app's page in the Google Play Store. The rating and review you submit are handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the app and the developer do not receive any information about whether or how you reviewed the app. The app keeps only a count of saves and the date it last showed the review window, on your device.

Advertising

The current version of the app does not display ads and does not use the advertising ID. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe the advertising provider, the information it handles, and the choices available to you.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not send your information to the developer or to third parties.

Your photos and other people's rights

Please use photos and artwork that you are allowed to use, for personal enjoyment. The developer does not see or check the images you use in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version is always published on this page, with the date it was last updated.
