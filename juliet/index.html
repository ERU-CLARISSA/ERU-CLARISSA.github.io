CROP • DECORATE • PRINT

Your favorite photos, made into photocards.

Juliet (Juliet Card on your home screen) is a photocard maker for Android. Crop your favorite photos and your own artwork to photocard size, decorate them with frames, names and your bias color, and lay them out on photo paper or A4, ready to print.

FEATURES

From photo to print-ready sheet.

Everything you need to make photocards at home or at a photo print shop.

01

Crop to photocard size

Trading card, B8, K-pop photocard (55×85 mm), instant photo style, ID photo style and more. Pinch and drag to fit.

02

Frames, text and bias color

Name bands, holo style, lace, ribbons, flowers, numbers and more. Change your bias color and the frame follows.

03

Print-ready sheets

4×6" (4R), 3.5×5" (3R / L), 5×7" (5R / 2L), A4, US Letter and postcard, with cut lines. Print at actual size from a PDF.

04

Safe from trimming

See the area that may be trimmed when printed, so faces and text stay safe. Brighten photos for printing.

05

Announcement images

Make an image with a reservation number and a QR code to share your sheet on social media.

06

Your photos stay with you

No account needed. Photos and sheets are stored on your device and leave it only when you choose to share them.

Available in English, 日本語, 한국어, 繁體中文, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino, Tiếng Việt, Español and Português.
