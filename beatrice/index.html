CHECK • CALENDAR • REPORT

Check it off, and watch your habits grow.

Beatrice (Betty Check on your home screen) is a habit tracker for Android. Check off your habits on a calendar, record morning, noon and night separately, and see how you are doing in clear reports — all in a design inspired by Italian Gothic art.

FEATURES

Simple to check, easy to keep going.

Everything you need to build habits that last.

01

Check on a calendar

Tap a day to check it off. Choose your own done mark — a flower, a star, a crown or any emoji you like.

02

Several checks a day

Set a daily goal such as 3 times a day, or check slots like morning, noon and night for cooking or medicine.

03

Every day or a few times a week

Aim for every day, or set a weekly goal such as 3 times a week. Streaks are counted in days or weeks.

04

Reports and heatmap

Totals, completion rates and comparisons with last week, month and year, with bar or line charts and a year heatmap.

05

Notes and reminders

Write a note for any day and search your notes later. Get reminders at the times and days you choose.

06

Your data stays with you

No account needed. Records are stored on your device. Save and restore backups, or export CSV and text files.

Available in English and 日本語.
