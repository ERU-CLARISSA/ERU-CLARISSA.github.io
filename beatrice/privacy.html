Privacy Policy

App: Beatrice — Habit Tracker (shown as "Betty Check" on the home screen; package name: com.eruclarissa.beatrice)
Developer: ERU CLARISSA
Last updated: October 29, 2026

Overview

Beatrice ("the app") is a habit tracker developed by ERU CLARISSA. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet to send your data, and the developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: the habits you create (names, notes, colors, done marks, daily and weekly goals, check slots, start dates and reminder times), your daily check records and the notes you write for each day, and your settings such as language, theme, first day of the week, chart style and reminder settings. This information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Home screen widget

If you add the app's home screen widget, the app stores the names of up to four habits and today's check status on your device so that the widget can show them. Checking a habit from the widget updates the records on your device only.

Backups and exports

If you choose to save a backup, or to export your records as a CSV or text file, the app creates a file and saves it to the location you select through your device's file picker. If you choose to restore a backup, the app reads only the file you select. The app does not upload these files anywhere; where you keep or share them is up to you.

Notifications and permissions

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and shows reminders at the times you choose. These reminders are scheduled on your device. The app also uses permission to run when the device starts, only so that scheduled reminders are restored after a restart.

Ratings and reviews

The app may show Google Play's in-app review window so that you can rate the app without leaving it. The rating and review you submit are handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the app and the developer do not receive any information about whether or how you reviewed the app.

Advertising

The current version of the app does not display ads and does not use the advertising ID. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe the advertising provider, the information it handles, and the choices available to you.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not send your information to the developer or to third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version is always published on this page, with the date it was last updated.
