TO DO • ON HOLD • DONE

Finish one task, and the next one appears.

Анна Каренина is a simple to-do list and task manager for Android. Keep your tasks in three tabs — what to do now, what can wait, and what is done — with quick due dates, repeating tasks and a daily reminder.

FEATURES

Simple to use, ready for real life.

Everything you need to keep track of what comes next.

01

Three tabs

To Do for now, On Hold for later, and Done for finished tasks — which you can bring back anytime.

02

Tasks in order

Link tasks as parent and subtask. A subtask waits on hold and moves to To Do when its parent is done.

03

Due dates in one tap

Today, Tomorrow, This week, Next week or This month — or pick a date and time. Choose which day ends the week for each category.

04

Repeating tasks

Daily, every few days, weekly, monthly or on chosen dates. Completed tasks come back on their next date, with a completion history.

05

Daily reminder

One notification a day at your chosen time, listing the tasks that are due soon.

06

Your data stays with you

No account needed. Tasks are stored on your device, and you can save and restore backups when you change phones.

Available in English and 日本語.
