Privacy Policy

App: Анна Каренина (Anna Karenina) — To-Do List & Tasks (package name: com.eruclarissa.annakarenina)

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

Анна Каренина ("the app") is a to-do list and task manager developed by ERU CLARISSA. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet to send your data, and the developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: the tasks you create (titles, notes, due dates, repeat settings, priority, difficulty, categories, links between tasks and completion history), your categories, and your settings such as sort order, time words and reminder settings. This information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Backups

If you choose to save a backup, the app creates a file containing your tasks, categories and settings, and saves it to the location you select through your device's file picker. If you choose to restore a backup, the app reads only the file you select. The app does not upload backups anywhere; where you keep or share a backup file is up to you.

Notifications and permissions

If you turn on deadline reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and shows one notification a day listing tasks that are due soon. These reminders are scheduled on your device. The app also uses permission to run when the device starts, only so that scheduled reminders are restored after a restart.

Advertising

The current version of the app does not display ads and does not use the advertising ID. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe the advertising provider, the information it handles, and the choices available to you.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not send your information to the developer or to third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version is always published on this page, with the date it was last updated.
