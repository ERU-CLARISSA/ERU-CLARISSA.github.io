Overview

CLARISSA — Focus Timer ("the app") is an Android interval and focus timer developed by ERU CLARISSA. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The app does not require an account, and the developer does not operate any server that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that your settings are restored the next time you open it: your intervals and repeat settings, your saved templates, theme and language settings, notification, sound and vibration settings, the completion sound you chose, and the background picture you selected together with its display position. This information is not sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Photos and sounds you choose

If you choose your own background picture, you select it through Android's file picker. The app only accesses the picture you choose, uses it to display the timer, and does not upload it anywhere. If you choose a completion sound, the app uses Android's sound picker and only stores a reference to the selected sound on your device.

Permissions

The app may ask for permission to show notifications. Notifications are used to show the remaining time and pause, resume, and reset buttons while the timer runs in the background. The app also uses vibration (if you turn it on) and a foreground service so that the timer keeps running when the screen is off.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by the developer, and is handled according to Google's policies:

• Google Privacy Policy
• How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Apart from the advertising described above, the app does not send your information to third parties.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version is always published on this page, with the date it was last updated.
