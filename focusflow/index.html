CLARISSA — Focus Timer is a flexible Android interval timer for study sessions, training, and any work-and-rest rhythm. Build the sequence you need, save it as your own template, and let the timer handle the transitions.

FEATURES

Designed around the way you work.

Simple at a glance, flexible when you need more control.

01

Custom intervals

Create up to 20 intervals with your own names and durations, from seconds to hours.

02

Your own templates

Start from built-in templates (Standard, Short, Study and Long), or save your current routine as a named template and switch in one tap.

03

Repeat your routine

Repeat a sequence 1–5 times, enter any count up to 999, or keep it running continuously.

04

Background timer

The timer keeps running with the screen off. Pause, resume, or reset right from the notification.

05

Alerts you control

Pick the completion sound from your device's sounds, or use vibration instead.

06

Personalize the look

Choose a theme color and a background picture — or use your own photo and adjust which part appears in the timer circle.
