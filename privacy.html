Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

FocusFlow is an Android interval and focus timer developed by ERU CLARISSA. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored by the app

FocusFlow stores timer preferences and app settings on your device so your routines, repeat settings, appearance preferences, and other choices can be restored.

Notifications and device features

If you grant the relevant Android permissions, FocusFlow may use notifications and vibration for timer status and interval-completion alerts.

Images and media

If you choose a background image through Android's file picker, the app uses that image for the app's visual display. FocusFlow does not require you to upload it to ERU CLARISSA.

Advertising

Advertising may be introduced in future versions. If third-party advertising is enabled, this policy will be updated to describe the provider, information handling, and available choices.

Data sharing

FocusFlow does not require an account to use the timer. We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when FocusFlow's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page.
